Good news for Cape Breton

Jesus Christ came into the world to save sinners.

The Gospel is God’s message of forgiveness, peace, and eternal life through His Son. It is good news for every person, in every community, and in every circumstance.

Discover the Gospel Visit a Gospel Hall
A sure word from God

“Believe on the Lord Jesus Christ, and thou shalt be saved.”

Acts 16:31

Salvation is not earned by religion or good works. It is received by faith in the Lord Jesus Christ and His finished work upon the cross.

A message worth hearing

God has made a way for sinners to be saved.

The Bible speaks honestly about our sin, clearly about God’s holiness, and wonderfully about the Saviour He provided. At Calvary, the Lord Jesus Christ died for our sins. He was buried, and He rose again the third day.

That finished work is the foundation of the Gospel. God now offers eternal life as a free gift to every person who trusts in His Son.

Gathered in His name

Simple gatherings centred on the Lord Jesus Christ

Gospel Hall assemblies are local companies of Christians who seek to gather according to the New Testament pattern, without a denominational name, and in dependence upon the Lord Jesus Christ.

Visitors are welcome. Meetings include the remembrance of the Lord, prayer, ministry from the Scriptures, and the public preaching of the Gospel.
